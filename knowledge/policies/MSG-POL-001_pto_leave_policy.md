## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Meridian Strategy Group (MSG) requirements for paid time off (PTO), including eligibility, planned leave, notice, approval, and the interaction between PTO and active client assignments.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to active MSG employees who receive PTO under their employment terms. It governs whether PTO may be requested. The process for submitting and routing a request is defined separately in the PTO Request & Approval Procedure.

## 3. Definitions

PTO means approved paid time away from work charged against the employee's available PTO balance. Planned PTO is foreseeable leave requested in advance. An active client assignment is an assignment whose status and dates show that the employee is staffed to a client engagement during the requested absence.

## 4. Eligibility

Active employees may request PTO when sufficient available PTO exists. A positive balance permits an employee to request leave but does not by itself constitute approval. PTO eligibility and the employee's current balance are separate determinations.

## 5. Planned PTO Notice

Standard planned PTO requires at least 14 calendar days' notice before the first requested PTO day. Requests made with less than 14 calendar days' notice are outside the standard planned-PTO rule and must not be represented as approved merely because sufficient balance exists.

## 6. PTO During Client Assignments

An employee may request PTO while assigned to an active client engagement. An active assignment does not automatically prohibit PTO. The employee remains responsible for following the applicable assignment approval route and for ensuring that the request is submitted through the designated PTO process.

## 7. Approval Requirements

Where the employee has an active client assignment covering the requested PTO dates, Engagement Manager approval is required. The identity of the applicable Engagement Manager is determined from the employee's active assignment record. Other requests follow the approval route defined by the PTO Request & Approval Procedure.

## 8. Insufficient Balance

A request that exceeds the employee's available PTO balance cannot be treated as eligible under the standard PTO provision. Meridian Compass must not infer additional entitlement from general practice or from an employee assertion.

## 9. Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for checking available balance, providing accurate dates, meeting notice requirements, and submitting the request. Approvers are responsible for acting on requests routed to them. People Operations maintains the governing policy and leave process.

## 10. Related Documents

MSG-PROC-001 PTO Request & Approval Procedure; MSG-POL-002 Assignment & Staffing Policy; MSG-POL-012 Employee Data Access & Privacy Policy.


## Key terms

- **planned PTO:** paid time off requested in advance under the PTO policy
- **available balance:** entitlement less used and pending days in the authorised PTO record
- **active assignment:** an assignment whose dates/status place the employee on current client work

Conversely, a policy requirement should not be converted into an invented workflow when no approved procedure describes the process.
