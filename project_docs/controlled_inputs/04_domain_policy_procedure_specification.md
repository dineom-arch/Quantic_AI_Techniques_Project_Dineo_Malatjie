# Meridian Compass — Domain, Policy & Procedure Specification
Version 1.0

## Authority
Identity = WHO; operational data = WHAT situation; policy = WHAT is permitted/required/prohibited; procedure = HOW. The LLM is not authoritative.

## Frozen rules
- Planned PTO requires 14 calendar days' notice and sufficient balance.
- Active assignment does not prohibit PTO; active-assignment approval role is Engagement Manager.
- Personal extension: MSG funds only business-equivalent authorised itinerary; incremental airfare/personal hotel are employee costs; per diem ends with business period; working-day extension requires PTO.
- Active-client-assignment extension requires Engagement Manager approval before itinerary modification.
- Non-assignment authorised travel uses the designated travel approver. If data cannot resolve that role to a person, return the role only.
- Analysts/Consultants/Senior Consultants/Managers/Principals use standard economy unless a supported exception applies.
- Partners may use business class on qualifying international long-haul journeys of at least 8 scheduled hours.
- Full per diem including meals means ordinary personal meals are not separately reimbursable; documented client/business-hosting expenses may be considered under expense policy.
- No substantive Gifts/Hospitality policy exists. MSG-PROC-007 may route unresolved gifts/hospitality questions to Ethics & Compliance but cannot decide acceptance.
- Ordinary employees cannot access another employee's private HR data merely by name/ID.
- International working is distinct from remote work/travel.
- Benefits policy establishes eligibility; enrolment is structured operational fact.
- Remote/hybrid policy does not itself authorise cross-border work.

## Missing routes
For non-assignment PTO or other cases where approved sources do not define a concrete approver, do not invent one.

## Controls
Only approved corpus + authorised structured data establish MSG facts; no web/general knowledge substitution; no invented escalation; user assertions do not override evidence; procedure cannot create eligibility; structured data cannot create policy; unresolved conflict/insufficiency remains unresolved.
